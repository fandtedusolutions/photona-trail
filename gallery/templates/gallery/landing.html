Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Photona's AI different?
Unlike basic tagging systems, Photona uses an advanced FAISS vector engine that matches faces instantly across tens of thousands of photos in milliseconds with near-perfect accuracy.
Can I bulk import photos via Google Drive?
Yes! Simply paste a public Google Drive folder link, and Photona will securely download, extract, and index all your event media entirely in the background.
How does attendee privacy work?
You have full control. You can lock entire events behind a PIN, or enforce a strict privacy mode where guests only receive photos their face was positively identified in.
Do I need to pay to test it out?
Not at all. You can start completely free to explore the platform, upload photos, and see the AI in action before deciding to upgrade your storage plan.
Can I download the processed photos?
Absolutely. Both you and your guests (if permitted) can download individual photos or download their entire personalized gallery as a ZIP file.
Is it optimized for mobile users?
Yes! The guest experience is designed entirely mobile-first, allowing them to snap a quick selfie on their phone and instantly view their matched photos without downloading any app.
